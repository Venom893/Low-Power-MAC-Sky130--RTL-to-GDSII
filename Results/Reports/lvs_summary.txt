--- Note ---
Device-level LVS confirms exact equivalence: all 431 real logic devices 
match between the physical layout and synthesized netlist, and all cell 
pin/port lists match exactly. The net-count discrepancy stems from a 
structural difference between logical and physical netlists: the 
Yosys/ORFS synthesized Verilog does not model power (VPWR/VGND) 
connectivity explicitly, while the magic-extracted layout does. This is 
a known, well-documented gap in open-source Sky130 flows, not a 
connectivity error in the design.
